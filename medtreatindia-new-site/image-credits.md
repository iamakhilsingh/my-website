# Treatment image provenance

The 16 images in `assets/treatments/` were created specifically for MedTreat India with OpenAI's built-in image-generation workflow on 2 August 2026. They do not reuse third-party stock photography, real patient photos, hospital branding, or identifiable real people.

Each image was prompted as original, natural editorial healthcare photography featuring Indian clinicians, patients, families, and contemporary Indian care environments. The set covers cardiology, heart surgery, orthopedics, knee replacement, hip replacement, cancer treatment, IVF and fertility, neurology, neurosurgery, liver transplant, kidney transplant, bone marrow transplant, dental treatment, eye treatment, spine surgery, and wellness and prevention.

Shared constraints across the prompt set: no text, logos, watermarks, flags, maps, brand marks, hospital signage, identifiable real people, graphic procedures, exposed anatomy, or medical distress. Each treatment uses a separately generated scene and a distinct source file.
