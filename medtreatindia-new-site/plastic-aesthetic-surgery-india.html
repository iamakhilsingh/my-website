Plastic & Aesthetic Surgery in India

Support for international patients exploring plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic procedures in India with clear specialist, hospital and recovery planning.

Start with clear goals and a specialist consultation

Plastic and aesthetic surgery planning should begin with an honest discussion of the patient’s goals, suitable options, potential risks, alternatives and realistic recovery expectations.

MedTreat India helps international patients coordinate specialist review, compare suitable hospitals and plan travel around consultations and recovery.

Common planning areas

Specialist fit

Match the doctor’s experience to the type of reconstructive or aesthetic procedure.

Realistic expectations

Discuss likely outcomes, limitations and whether the procedure suits the patient.

Written treatment plan

Request clear details about the procedure, hospital stay and package inclusions.

Recovery timing

Plan enough time for post-procedure reviews before return travel.

Responsible planning matters

The treating surgeon should provide case-specific medical advice after consultation. Before travelling, confirm the proposed procedure, anaesthesia plan, expected recovery period, follow-up needs and fitness-to-fly guidance.
